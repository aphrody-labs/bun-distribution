## Comparisons and decisions

Method for PostgreSQL: each row is hashed as `md5(to_jsonb(row))` with TimeZone=UTC, and the multisets are compared.
The remote side is the fresh `pg_dump` restored on WSL. Method for SQLite: `EXCEPT` over each table's columns, with
`vec0`/FTS virtual tables compared through their shadow tables.

- **aphrody, aphrody_web, stack (WSL vs vps):** 0 rows differ on either side. The WSL copies were exact duplicates and were dropped.
- **rg (WSL vs vps):** 14,299 row versions exist only on WSL and 16,319 only on vps, across 27 tables. WSL holds 3 `inbound_emails` rows from 2026-10-01 that are absent on vps, plus local Discord/campaign sync states. Neither copy is a superset. The WSL copy is kept as the dev mirror and dumped into `wsl/`.
- **shenron_site (WSL vs dbfr):** dbfr has 821 extra rows. The 4 rows found only on WSL are older versions of rows that dbfr holds with a newer `updatedAt` or `asked_count`. The dbfr copy is the most complete. The WSL copy is kept as the ADR D3 dev mirror, because `scripts/dev/shenron/scripts/dev/local-env.ts` uses it.
- **achillea vs rg (`achillea.*` schema):** all 69 business tables exist in rg. Every business row's primary key is present in rg, except `guild_member_meta`: 82 of 3,345 keys are absent (rg's Discord sync dropped members who left). Other differences are newer row versions in rg, such as `user` 446, `clan` 69, `guild_role` 105. Not migrated: Achillea's own GoTrue auth (1 user, 2 sessions, 3 refresh tokens, 3 flow states, 2 MFA claims, 1 identity), 7 `storage.migrations` bookkeeping rows, and the `realtime`/`supabase_functions` migration tables. Following the owner procedure, the final dump `vps/postgres/retired/achillea-final-20261001T185119Z.dump` keeps all of this, and the database was dropped.
- **Retired DBs:** each final dump is row-identical (0/0) to the 18:01 dump and to the 03:15 nightly. The 18:01 copies were therefore removed from the backup set, and the 03:15 nightlies were deleted.
- **Shenron `bot.db`:** the live `vps:/srv/shenron/shared/bot/data/bot.db` is the most complete (109 tables, 578,453 rows at inventory). Every older snapshot holds some row versions that are absent from live:
  - dbfr replica: 6,307.
  - vps nightlies: 6,314 to 6,607.
  - pre-resync snapshot: 135,932.
  - vps legacy checkout copy: 169,849.

  Because none of them is a strict subset, every older snapshot was archived byte-for-byte in `history/` before deletion, instead of being treated as superseded.
- **OCR `results.db.prev` (dbfr):** 0 rows are absent from `results.db`, so it is a strict subset and was deleted. `results.db` is in `sqlite-current`.
- **bxc crawls (vps):** each crawl holds rows that no other crawl has, including consolidated-v4. All of them are kept. WAL-safe snapshots are in `sqlite-current`. v2, v3 and v4 were already hardlinked.
- **Byte-identical groups:**

## Actions on the servers

### Database retirement (vps, owner decision, ADR D3)

Each database went through the same procedure:

1. `pg_stat_activity` was sampled 9 times over 3 minutes. The only session was the `pg_net` background worker in `achillea`.
2. Running units and their environment files were checked; they reference only `rg` and `shenron_site`.
3. `REVOKE CONNECT`, then client backends were terminated.
4. A final `pg_dump -Fc` and `pg_dumpall --globals-only` were taken and checked with `pg_restore --list` and sha256.
5. `DROP DATABASE … WITH (FORCE)`.
