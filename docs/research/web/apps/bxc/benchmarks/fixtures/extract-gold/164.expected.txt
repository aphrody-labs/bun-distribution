Weeknight lentil and spinach curry
This curry started as a way to use up half a bag of red lentils and a wilting box of spinach, and it has since become the dish my family requests most on busy evenings. It takes about thirty-five minutes, most of which is simmering.
Red lentils break down as they cook, which makes the sauce thick and creamy without any cream. If you prefer more texture, stir in a tin of chickpeas for the last ten minutes.
The spice list looks long, but it is mostly things that live in the cupboard for months. If you only have curry powder, use two tablespoons and skip the individual spices.
Ingredients
250 g red lentils, rinsed
1 onion, finely chopped
3 garlic cloves, grated
1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
2 tsp ground cumin
1 tsp ground turmeric
1 tsp garam masala
400 ml tin coconut milk
600 ml vegetable stock
150 g fresh spinach
Juice of half a lemon
Instructions
Soften the onion in a little oil over medium heat for eight minutes, until golden at the edges.
Add the garlic, ginger and spices and cook for one minute, stirring so nothing sticks.
Tip in the lentils, coconut milk and stock. Bring to a simmer and cook for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the lentils have collapsed.
Stir in the spinach a handful at a time until wilted, then season with salt and the lemon juice.
Notes
The curry keeps for four days in the fridge and thickens as it cools; loosen it with a splash of water when reheating.
