Best way to store sourdough starter during a three-week trip?
I am leaving for three weeks and nobody can feed my starter. Should I refrigerate it, dry it, or freeze it? It is a 100 percent hydration rye starter that is about two years old.
Three weeks in the fridge is fine. Give it a stiff feed at 50 percent hydration right before you leave so it has plenty of food, and put it in a jar with a loose lid.
When you get back it will look grey and smell like nail polish. Pour off the liquid, feed it twice a day for two days and it will recover.
If you want insurance, smear a thin layer on baking paper, let it dry completely and crumble it into a bag. Dried starter keeps for months and revives in about four days.
Did both, just in case. The refrigerated one bubbled again after the second feed. Thanks!
