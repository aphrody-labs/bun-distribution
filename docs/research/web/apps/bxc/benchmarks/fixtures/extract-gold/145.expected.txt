Lumen S2 Desk Lamp
The Lumen S2 uses a wide, diffused LED panel that lights a whole desk without the harsh hot spot of a single bulb. Colour temperature adjusts from warm 2700 K for evenings to neutral 5000 K for detailed work.
An ambient light sensor dims the lamp gradually as daylight increases, and the arm folds flat when you need the desk space back.
Features
Flicker-free dimming
USB-C charging port in the base
Memory function for last brightness
Aluminium arm with matte finish
Specifications
Brightness 850 lumens
Colour rendering CRI 95
Power 12 W
Height 38–62 cm
