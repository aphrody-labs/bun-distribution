Slow-roasted tomato and white bean soup
Roasting tomatoes for an hour concentrates their flavour so much that even supermarket tomatoes in February taste like August. The beans add body, and a spoonful of pesto at the end brings everything together.
Ingredients
1 kg ripe tomatoes, halved
1 red onion, quartered
4 garlic cloves, unpeeled
2 tbsp olive oil
400 g tin cannellini beans, drained
700 ml vegetable stock
Basil pesto to serve
Instructions
Roast the tomatoes, onion and garlic with the oil at 180 °C for one hour.
Squeeze the garlic from its skins and blend everything with half the beans and the stock.
Add the remaining beans, simmer for ten minutes and season to taste.
Serve with a spoonful of pesto and crusty bread.
Notes
The soup freezes well for up to three months.
