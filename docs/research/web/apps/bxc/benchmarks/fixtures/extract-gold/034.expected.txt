Proving your identity with the GOV.UK One Login app
How to use the GOV.UK One Login app to prove your identity, including how to download the app and how to scan your face and photo ID.
You can use the GOV.UK One Login app to prove your identity when you sign in to a government service with GOV.UK One Login.
To use the app, you must start on the government service you are trying to access. You’ll be guided to the app when it’s time to use it.
The app will check that:
your photo ID is real
you are a real person
you are the same person as in your photo ID
If you prefer not to use the app, you can prove your identity in another way.
What you’ll need
You’ll need access to a phone with a working camera.
If you’re using an iPhone, it must be:
an iPhone 8 or newer
running iOS 16.7 or higher
For an Android phone (for example, Samsung or Google Pixel), it must be running Android 10 or higher.
Types of photo ID you can use in the app
You’ll need one of the following types of photo ID:
UK photocard driving licence
UK passport
non-UK passport with a biometric chip
UK biometric residence permit (BRP)
UK biometric residence card (BRC)
UK Frontier Worker permit (FWP)
If your photo ID has expired
You cannot use an expired passport to prove your identity with the app.
You can use an expired driving licence if it expired in the last 90 days and it was issued by DVLA.
You can use an expired BRP, BRC or FWP if it expired in the last 24 months.
Downloading the app
You’ll be told when to download the app.
If you need help to download the app to your phone, there is advice on:
downloading an app to an iPhone
downloading an app to an Android phone
Signing in to the app
After downloading the app, you’ll need to sign in with your GOV.UK One Login details. This will link the GOV.UK website to your app so you can continue proving your identity.
If you’re having trouble signing in to the app, read guidance on how to change your GOV.UK One Login sign in details.
Choosing your photo ID
You’ll need to choose which photo ID you’ll use to prove your identity. Make sure you have your original photo ID with you. You cannot use a photocopy or digital copy.
Once you’ve confirmed the photo ID you’ll use, you cannot go back and use a different one.
If you need to change to a different type of photo ID, you’ll need to go back to the service you were trying to use and start proving your identity again.
Scanning your photo ID
If you’re using a passport or a BRP, BRC or FWP
If you’re using a passport, make sure it has a biometric chip. To check if your passport has a biometric chip, look for the rectangular biometric chip symbol on the front cover.
You’ll be asked to:
Take a photo of your photo ID
Scan the biometric chip in your photo ID using your phone.
Scan your face using your phone.
If the biometric scan did not work, it can be because:
your phone was not touching your photo ID
your phone had a case or accessory on it - try removing these and scanning your photo ID again
internet connection has been lost briefly
the phone was moved while the scan was happening
you scanned another document, other than your passport or residence permit
You could try again and you should see a help animation. Follow the on-screen instructions and, when you’re ready, select ‘Start scan’ or place your phone on top of your passport to start the scan automatically.
If you’re using a UK driving licence
You’ll be asked to take a photo of your driving licence. The photo will be taken automatically, so do not touch your screen while this is happening.
Make sure you:
hold your driving licence in the palm of one hand and your phone with the other hand - if you’re having trouble taking a photo of the licence while it’s in your hand, place it on a dark matte background
move the licence away from bright sources of light to reduce glare
get your entire licence inside the white frame
Scanning your face
You’ll use your front-facing (selfie) camera to do this.
When you scan your face with the GOV.UK One Login app, make sure:
your face is completely aligned with the oval on your screen
you are looking straight ahead
you keep as still as you can while the scan happens
How to finish proving your identity
Once you’ve scanned your face with the app and your information has been submitted, you’ll need to return to the GOV.UK page you left open on your web browser. You’ll then be able to finish proving your identity and continue to the service you were trying to access.
What to do if the identity check on the app does not work
If you were not able to prove your identity with the first photo ID you’ve chosen, you can try again using another photo ID.
Go back to the service you were trying to access to start proving your identity again.
You can also try to prove your identity another way.
