Frequently asked questions
Is my banking data secure?
Ledgerly connects through regulated open banking providers and never stores your banking password. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
Can I import previous years?
Yes. Upload CSV exports from your bank or previous software and Ledgerly will categorise the history using the same rules.
What happens if I cancel?
You can export all reports and transactions at any time, and your data remains available for ninety days after cancellation.
