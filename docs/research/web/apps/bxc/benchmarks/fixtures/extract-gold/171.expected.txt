Household energy use by region, 2024
This release reports average annual electricity and gas consumption per household for each region, based on metered data from distribution network operators. Figures are weather-corrected to allow comparison with previous years.

Methodology
Consumption is estimated from annual meter readings for domestic meter points. Households with rooftop solar panels are included, which lowers the recorded electricity consumption in regions with high solar adoption.
