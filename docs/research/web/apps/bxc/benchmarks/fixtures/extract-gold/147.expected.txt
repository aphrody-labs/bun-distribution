Northwind 40L Hiking Backpack
The Northwind 40L is built for long weekends in the mountains, when a daypack is too small and an expedition pack is too heavy. Its adjustable back system fits torso lengths from 42 to 53 centimetres, and the ventilated mesh panel keeps air moving between your back and the load.
The main compartment opens from the top and from the front, so you can reach a jacket at the bottom without unpacking your lunch. A separate sleeve holds a hydration bladder of up to three litres.
Recycled ripstop nylon with water-repellent coating
Integrated rain cover in the base pocket
Hip belt pockets large enough for a phone
Two ice axe loops and trekking pole holders
Volume 40 litres
Weight 1.35 kg
Back length 42–53 cm
