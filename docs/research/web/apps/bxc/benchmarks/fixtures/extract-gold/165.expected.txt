Crisp oven-baked potato wedges
The secret to wedges that are crisp outside and fluffy inside is to parboil them briefly and rough up the edges before they go in the oven. The broken surface creates more crunchy edges.
I use floury potatoes such as Maris Piper or russet. Waxy potatoes hold their shape too well and never develop the same crust.
Ingredients
1 kg floury potatoes, cut into wedges
3 tbsp olive oil
1 tsp smoked paprika
1 tsp garlic powder
Flaky salt
Chopped parsley to serve
Instructions
Heat the oven to 220 °C and put an empty roasting tray inside to heat up.
Boil the wedges in salted water for six minutes, drain, and leave to steam dry for five minutes.
Shake the wedges in the colander to rough up the edges, then toss with oil, paprika and garlic powder.
Spread them on the hot tray in a single layer and roast for 35 minutes, turning once, until deep golden.
Notes
Crowding the tray makes the wedges steam instead of roast; use two trays if needed.
