i think i know whats going on, the wiki tells you to put the "Server" entry in /etc/pacman.conf , but doesnt even mention removing the other entries which are pointing to /etc/pacman.d/mirrorlist.
please remove the "Server = " line you added to your /etc/pacman.conf and add it to the bottom of your /etc/pacman.d/mirrorlist and comment out the other entries like i did above
nothing is "fucked" dont worry
EDIT:
i just realised you probably have a full length mirrorlist file, i keep forgetting mines custom, better idea is rename your mirrorlist file
sudo mv /etc/pacman.d/mirrorlist /etc/pacman.d/mirrorlist.bak
then create a new one with just that one server line
sudo su
echo 'Server=https://archive.archlinux.org/repos/2026/09/10/$repo/os/$arch' > /etc/pacman.d/mirrorlist
then when your ready to update from the current repos again just reinstate the original mirrorlist by changing the names again, simple as !
