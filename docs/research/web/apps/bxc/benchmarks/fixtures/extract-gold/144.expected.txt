Aurelia Cast Iron Casserole 26 cm
Enamelled cast iron holds heat evenly and releases it slowly, which makes the Aurelia casserole ideal for braises, stews and bread baked inside a closed pot. The light interior enamel lets you watch browning without guessing.
The lid has small studs underneath that collect condensation and drip it back onto the food, keeping slow-cooked dishes moist without opening the pot.
Features
Suitable for all hobs including induction
Oven safe up to 250 °C with the lid knob
Chip-resistant enamel
Made in France
Specifications
Diameter 26 cm
Capacity 5.3 litres
Weight 5.4 kg
Dishwasher safe Yes, hand washing recommended
