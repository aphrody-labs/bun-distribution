# Hosted service scope

Hosted multi-tenant delivery is deferred behind the local coding-agent
product and its [release acceptance](./PLAN.md#release-acceptance).
[PLAN.md](./PLAN.md) is the only active execution plan. No tenant API, account,
billing or public deployment readiness is established by this document.

A future hosted proposal must name its owner, authentication and tenant
boundaries, execution isolation, quotas, retention, licensing and restore
proof. Hosts, routes and deployment state come from
[aphrody-infra](../../products/infra/README.md); the public web origin belongs to
[aphrody-web](../../products/web/README.md). The local full-access coding slice
is not a multi-tenant execution policy.

The previous infrastructure snapshots, proposed domains, service choices and
commercial phases are superseded. They remain available in the
[historical proposal at bb46076](https://github.com/aphrody-labs/aphrody/blob/bb46076ce7ab57ea3af96486dbefc66392ef3562/docs/SAAS-FUTURE-PLAN.md).
Reassess them against the active product and live infra catalog before
starting any hosted implementation.
